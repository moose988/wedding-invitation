## Prompts (abridged)

- **Closed chest**: front view of an antique Gulf mandoos, dark teak with rows
  of brass studs in diamond and chevron patterns, pierced brass corners, a
  round brass lock plate in the exact centre; emerald velvet, pearls, jasmine.
- **Open chest**: top-down into the chest lined with emerald velvet, a large
  empty centre; pearls, a gold murtaasha necklace and hair ornaments, a talli
  braid coil, a henna cone, a small incense burner, rose petals at the edges.
- **Henna hands**: bridal Gulf henna, palms cupping one white pearl, emerald
  sleeves edged with talli.
- **Gahwa**: brass dallah pouring Arabic coffee into a finjan, dates, sadu runner.
- **Mabkhara**: carved wooden incense burner with a tall ribbon of smoke.
- **Opening film**: the lock gleams, the lid lifts, candlelight spills out, the
  camera rises and settles looking straight down into the empty velvet centre.
- **Loops**: locked-off camera; only smoke, candle flame and the sheen on
  pearls and gold move.
