## Prompts (abridged)

- **Card**: overhead editorial flat-lay, blank deckled pomegranate-wine cotton
  card on blush lime plaster, copper silk ribbon, halved pomegranate, coral
  blossoms, hammered copper dish, late-afternoon window light, no text.
- **Courtyard**: symmetrical dusk view of an empty Andalusian riad set for a
  wedding: octagonal zellige fountain, pomegranate trees in terracotta,
  horseshoe arches in blush plaster, copper lanterns, petals, wine silk
  drapes, plum sky; 35 mm film look, no people or text.
- **Table**: candlelit place setting, terracotta plate, copper cutlery,
  wine napkin tied with a pomegranate blossom, garnet tapers.
- **Botanicals**: vintage botanical plates in wine ink with garnet, coral and
  sage watercolour on plain blush paper (a sprig; an open wreath).
- **Opening film**: ribbon slips, petals drift, camera glides into the card
  until its colour fills the frame, which opens like parting silk drapes onto
  the candlelit courtyard.
- **Loops**: locked-off camera; candles flicker, fountain ripples, leaves and
  drapes move slightly, a few petals fall.
